# 1. Purpose

This Architecture Decision Record (ADR) documents an important architectural decision made for the Wealth Path AI Global (WPAG) platform.

Each ADR captures:

- The architectural problem or context.
- The decision that was made.
- The reasoning behind the decision.
- The expected consequences.
- Any future considerations.

The objective is to preserve institutional knowledge and ensure architectural consistency throughout the lifecycle of the platform.
